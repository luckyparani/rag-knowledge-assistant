Maternity Leave Policy
Employees who have completed 1 year of continuous service are eligible for maternity leave. The leave duration is 26 weeks with full pay. Employees must apply at least 30 days in advance.

Sick Leave Policy
All employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave can be taken without prior notice in case of emergencies. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance through the HR portal.

Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after approval.
